# General Suggestions for PrOREposals

**For a proposal to make sense, it needs to solve a problem.** The more problems the proposal can solve with the smallest and cheapest (least amount of work) the better. Ultimately, the proposal will be judged by how well it will be accepted by the community *and* staff. So the Proposal must present a solution to the problems that we want to solve in a way that *we, the community* want it solved. What problems do we want to solve then?
For a proposal to be successful as a proposal, though not necessarily for being chosen, it needs to show what problem it is solving, how that problem manifests now, what the ideal solution (of this proposal) would look like, as well as how to implement it. This is called a *GAP Analysis*. It shows you the GAP between where you *currently are* and where you *want to be in the future*. Once you understand that gap and have a list of everything that needs to be changed, you can make a plan for how that can be implemented.
Your proposal is this. The Gap Analysis, what Requirements it fulfills, as well as your preliminary Plan for how to implement this change. The proposal should fully explain the necessity for any change, the impact on the community as well as possible other solutions to problems. A full workflow for the implementation of the proposal should be included as well.

> [!Note]
> Those of you who are interested in becoming engineers should take note of this, as well as the terminology, for many of you will, willingly or not, need to deal with a not insignificant amount of Project Management. This is a part of it. Making good and successful proposals is how you will get your engineering projects funded, and ultimately made.
